# Article 63 — Consistency Mechanism

**Chapter:** 7 — Cooperation and Consistency

## Summary
Article 63 is the gateway provision for the consistency mechanism set out in Section 2 of Chapter 7 (Articles 63–67). It states that, in order to contribute to the consistent application of the GDPR throughout the Union, supervisory authorities shall cooperate with each other and, where relevant, with the Commission through the consistency mechanism. The article itself is brief and introductory — the substantive procedures are found in Articles 64 (opinions), 65 (dispute resolution), and 66 (urgency procedure). The consistency mechanism ensures that decisions with cross-border implications are not taken unilaterally by a single SA but are subject to supranational scrutiny through the EDPB.

## Key Obligations
- Supervisory authorities must **use the consistency mechanism** for measures intended to produce effects in multiple Member States.
- Cooperation must extend to **the Commission** where relevant.
- SAs may **not adopt final decisions** on matters that have been, or should be, submitted to the consistency mechanism until the procedure is concluded.

## Key Rights / Definitions
- **Consistency mechanism**: The set of procedures (Articles 64–67) through which the EDPB ensures uniform GDPR application by issuing opinions, binding decisions, and urgent measures.

## Applies To
Supervisory authorities, the European Data Protection Board, the Commission.

## Todo

- [ ] In exceptional circumstances where urgent action is needed, adopt provisional measures on own territory with a specified validity period (maximum three months)
- [ ] Without delay, communicate the provisional measures and reasons for adopting them to other concerned SAs, the EDPB, and the Commission
- [ ] Where provisional measures have been adopted and final measures are urgently needed, request an urgent opinion or urgent binding decision from the EDPB
- [ ] Any SA may request an urgent Board opinion or binding decision where a competent SA has not acted and urgent protection of data subjects' rights is required
- [ ] Ensure that urgent opinions or binding decisions are sought within the two-week deadline framework (Article 66(4))
- [ ] Note that provisional measures expire automatically after three months if no further action is taken

# Article 66 — Urgency Procedure

**Chapter:** 7 — Cooperation and Consistency

## Summary
Article 66 provides a safety valve for situations requiring immediate action to protect data subjects. By derogation from the normal cooperation and consistency procedures (Articles 60, 63–65), a supervisory authority concerned may immediately adopt provisional measures on its own territory with a maximum validity of three months. The SA must immediately notify the other concerned SAs, the EDPB, and the Commission. If the SA considers that final measures are urgently needed, it may request an urgent opinion or urgent binding decision from the EDPB. Any SA may also request such an urgent decision where a competent SA has failed to act. The EDPB must issue urgent opinions or binding decisions within two weeks by simple majority — a much shorter and lower-threshold procedure than under Articles 64 and 65.

## Key Obligations
- Provisional measures: valid for **maximum three months**; immediate notification to other SAs, EDPB, and Commission required.
- Provisional measures are adopted **by derogation** from the standard cooperation (Article 60) and consistency (Articles 63–65) procedures.
- Urgent EDPB opinion or binding decision must be issued within **two weeks by simple majority**.
- Any SA (not only the one that adopted provisional measures) may request urgent Board action where a competent SA has **failed to act**.

## Key Rights / Definitions
- **Provisional measures**: Temporary protective orders adopted on a SA's own territory without going through the normal cooperation or consistency procedure; automatically expire after three months.
- **Urgent opinion/binding decision**: An accelerated EDPB output issued within two weeks where normal timelines (8 weeks / 1–2 months) would be insufficient to protect data subjects.

## Applies To
Supervisory authorities, the European Data Protection Board.

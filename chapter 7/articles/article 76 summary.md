# Article 76 — Confidentiality

**Chapter:** 7 — Cooperation and Consistency

## Summary
Article 76 addresses the confidentiality of EDPB proceedings and access to Board documents. Board discussions may be confidential where the Board determines this is necessary in accordance with its rules of procedure. Access to documents submitted to Board members, experts, and representatives of third parties is governed by Regulation (EC) No 1049/2001, which sets out the rules for public access to European Parliament, Council, and Commission documents and is applied by analogy to the EDPB as a Union body. This provision balances the need for open deliberation (particularly on sensitive enforcement matters) with the transparency and public accountability principles that underpin the GDPR's governance framework.

## Key Obligations
- Board discussions **may be confidential** where the Board's rules of procedure so provide.
- Access to documents submitted to Board members, experts, and third-party representatives is governed by **Regulation (EC) No 1049/2001**.

## Key Rights / Definitions
- **Confidential discussions**: Internal Board deliberations that the Board has determined should be protected from public disclosure, as specified in the rules of procedure.
- **Regulation (EC) No 1049/2001**: The EU public access to documents regulation; applies to the EDPB as a Union body, allowing the public to request access to Board documents subject to defined exceptions.

## Applies To
The European Data Protection Board, members of the Board, experts, third-party representatives.

# Article 70 — Tasks of the Board

**Chapter:** 7 — Cooperation and Consistency

## Summary
Article 70 sets out the full catalogue of the EDPB's tasks. The overarching objective is to ensure the consistent application of the GDPR across the Union. The Board acts on its own initiative or at the request of the Commission. Its tasks include: monitoring GDPR application (Articles 64–65), advising the Commission on amendments and policy, issuing guidelines/recommendations/best practices on a wide range of topics (DPIA procedures, right to erasure, breach notification, profiling, BCRs, derogations, fines, common infringement reporting procedures), reviewing those guidelines in practice, approving certification criteria and accreditation requirements, issuing adequacy opinions, adopting binding dispute-resolution decisions, promoting SA cooperation and training, and maintaining a public register of consistency-mechanism decisions. The Board publishes all its outputs and, where appropriate, conducts public consultations.

## Key Obligations
- Issue **guidelines, recommendations, and best practices** on the topics enumerated in Article 70(1).
- Issue **opinions** under Article 64 and adopt **binding decisions** under Articles 65 and 66.
- **Review** the practical application of issued guidelines and best practices.
- **Maintain a public register** of SA and court decisions under the consistency mechanism.
- **Consult interested parties** and publish consultation results where appropriate.
- Forward all outputs to the Commission and Article 93 committee and **make them public**.

## Key Rights / Definitions
- **Guidelines/Recommendations/Best Practices**: Soft-law outputs of the EDPB; not formally binding but carry significant de facto authority and are widely relied upon for compliance purposes.
- **Binding decisions**: Formal decisions under Articles 65 and 66 that legally bind concerned SAs and form the basis of final national decisions.

## Applies To
The European Data Protection Board, supervisory authorities, the Commission, controllers, processors.

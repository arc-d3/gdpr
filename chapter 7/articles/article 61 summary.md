# Article 61 — Mutual Assistance

**Chapter:** 7 — Cooperation and Consistency

## Summary
Article 61 creates a general mutual assistance obligation between supervisory authorities. Each SA must provide relevant information and assistance to other SAs to ensure consistent implementation of the GDPR. Requests must be answered within one month. The requested SA may refuse only if it lacks competence for the subject-matter or if compliance would breach Union or Member State law. It must provide reasons for any refusal. Information exchanged is limited-purpose and confidential. No fees may be charged. Where a requested SA fails to respond within one month, the requesting SA may adopt provisional measures on its own territory and the urgency procedure under Article 66 is triggered. The Commission may adopt implementing acts standardising the electronic format for information exchange.

## Key Obligations
- SAs must provide **relevant information and mutual assistance** to other SAs.
- Requests must be replied to **within one month** of receipt.
- Requests must include sufficient information (purpose, reasons); exchanged information is **purpose-limited**.
- Requested SA may refuse only where it lacks **competence** or compliance would **infringe this Regulation or applicable law**.
- Requested SA must give **reasons for refusal**.
- Information is supplied **electronically in a standardised format** as a rule.
- **No fee** may be charged; exceptional expenditure may be subject to indemnity agreements.
- Failure to respond within one month entitles the requesting SA to adopt **provisional measures** under Article 55(1) and triggers Article 66 urgency review.

## Key Rights / Definitions
- **Mutual assistance**: Information requests, supervisory measures, prior authorisations, consultations, inspections, and investigations conducted at another SA's request.
- **Purpose limitation**: Information obtained through mutual assistance may only be used for the purpose for which it was requested.

## Applies To
Supervisory authorities.

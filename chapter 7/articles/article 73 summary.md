# Article 73 — Chair

**Chapter:** 7 — Cooperation and Consistency

## Summary
Article 73 governs the election of the EDPB's Chair and two Deputy Chairs. All three are elected from among the Board's members (i.e., heads of national SAs or their representatives) by a simple majority of those members. Each term of office is five years and may be renewed once, giving a maximum tenure of ten years for any individual in the same position. The Deputy Chairs assist the Chair and, under the Board's rules of procedure, take on designated tasks. The Chair's specific responsibilities are detailed in Article 74.

## Key Obligations
- Elect a **Chair** and **two Deputy Chairs** by simple majority from Board members.
- Term: **five years**, renewable **once**.

## Key Rights / Definitions
- **Chair**: The elected leader of the EDPB who represents the Board externally (Article 68(2)) and performs the specific tasks listed in Article 74.
- **Deputy Chairs**: Two elected Board members who assist the Chair and assume tasks allocated under the Board's rules of procedure.

## Applies To
The European Data Protection Board members.

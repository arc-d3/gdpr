# Article 68 — European Data Protection Board

**Chapter:** 7 — Cooperation and Consistency

## Summary
Article 68 formally establishes the European Data Protection Board (EDPB) as a body of the European Union with legal personality. The Board is composed of the heads (or their representatives) of one supervisory authority from each Member State plus the European Data Protection Supervisor. Where a Member State has multiple SAs, a joint representative is appointed under national law. The Commission has the right to participate in Board activities and meetings without a voting right. The Board is represented externally by its Chair. The EDPS has voting rights only in Article 65 cases concerning rules applicable to EU institutions, bodies, offices, and agencies. The EDPB replaced the Article 29 Working Party upon the GDPR's application on 25 May 2018.

## Key Obligations
- Each Member State must ensure its SA head (or representative) **sits on the Board**.
- Member States with multiple SAs must **appoint a joint representative**.
- The Commission must **designate a representative** to participate in Board activities.
- The Board Chair must **inform the Commission** of the Board's activities.

## Key Rights / Definitions
- **European Data Protection Board (EDPB)**: The independent EU body established by Article 68, replacing the Article 29 Working Party, with legal personality and decision-making powers under Chapter 7.
- **European Data Protection Supervisor (EDPS)**: The independent supervisory authority for EU institutions and bodies; also a full voting member of the EDPB (with restricted voting rights in Article 65 dispute resolution cases).
- **Legal personality**: The EDPB can enter into legal relations, own property, and be party to legal proceedings in its own name.

## Applies To
Member States, supervisory authorities, the European Data Protection Supervisor, the Commission.

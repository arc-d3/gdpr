# Article 72 — Procedure

**Chapter:** 7 — Cooperation and Consistency

## Summary
Article 72 establishes the default voting rule for the EDPB: decisions are taken by simple majority of members, unless the Regulation provides otherwise. Notable exceptions requiring a higher threshold include: binding decisions under Article 65 (two-thirds majority for the first vote, simple majority as fallback); and adoption of rules of procedure (two-thirds majority under Article 72(2) itself). The Board must also adopt its own rules of procedure by a two-thirds majority and organise its operational arrangements accordingly. This article ensures procedural clarity and democratic legitimacy in the Board's decision-making.

## Key Obligations
- Default decision rule: **simple majority** of Board members.
- Rules of procedure: adopted by **two-thirds majority**.
- Special voting thresholds apply where the Regulation specifically provides (e.g., Article 65(2)).

## Key Rights / Definitions
- **Simple majority**: More than half of the votes cast by members present; the standard EDPB decision threshold.
- **Two-thirds majority**: Required for adopting rules of procedure and for first-round binding decisions under Article 65.

## Applies To
The European Data Protection Board.

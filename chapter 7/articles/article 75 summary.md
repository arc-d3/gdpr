# Article 75 — Secretariat

**Chapter:** 7 — Cooperation and Consistency

## Summary
Article 75 establishes the EDPB secretariat, which is provided by the European Data Protection Supervisor. The secretariat acts exclusively under the instructions of the Chair of the Board, not the EDPS, ensuring its operational independence from the EDPS's own supervisory functions. EDPS staff working on EDPB tasks must have separate reporting lines from those performing EDPS tasks. Where appropriate, the Board and the EDPS must conclude a Memorandum of Understanding governing their cooperation. The secretariat's responsibilities cover the full spectrum of EDPB operations: day-to-day business, internal and external communications, translation, meeting preparation and follow-up, and drafting and publishing EDPB outputs (opinions, decisions, and other texts).

## Key Obligations
- Secretariat provided by the **EDPS** but operates exclusively under the **Chair's instructions**.
- EDPS staff for EDPB tasks must have **separate reporting lines** from EDPS-function staff.
- Board and EDPS should establish a **Memorandum of Understanding** where appropriate.
- Secretariat performs: day-to-day operations, communications, translations, meeting preparation, and drafting of EDPB outputs.

## Key Rights / Definitions
- **Secretariat**: The administrative body supporting the EDPB's functioning; staffed by EDPS personnel under the Chair's authority.
- **Memorandum of Understanding**: An agreement between the Board and the EDPS to operationalise Article 75's division of responsibilities and staff management.

## Applies To
The European Data Protection Board, the European Data Protection Supervisor, the Chair of the Board.

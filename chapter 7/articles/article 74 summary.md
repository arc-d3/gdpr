# Article 74 — Tasks of the Chair

**Chapter:** 7 — Cooperation and Consistency

## Summary
Article 74 defines the specific tasks of the EDPB Chair. The Chair is responsible for convening Board meetings and preparing the agenda, notifying binding decisions under Article 65 to the lead SA and all concerned SAs, and ensuring that the Board's tasks are performed in a timely manner — particularly in relation to the consistency mechanism (Article 63). The allocation of tasks between the Chair and Deputy Chairs is to be set out in the Board's rules of procedure. The Chair also communicates EDPB activities to the Commission (Article 68(5)) and notifies the Commission of binding decisions (Article 65(5)).

## Key Obligations
- **Convene meetings** and **prepare the agenda**.
- **Notify Article 65 binding decisions** to the lead SA and all concerned SAs.
- **Ensure timely performance** of Board tasks, especially under the consistency mechanism.
- **Allocate tasks** with Deputy Chairs via rules of procedure.

## Key Rights / Definitions
- **Chair's notification obligation**: Upon adoption of a binding decision under Article 65, the Chair must without undue delay inform all relevant SAs and the Commission.

## Applies To
The Chair of the European Data Protection Board.

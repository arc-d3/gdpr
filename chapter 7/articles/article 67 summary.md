# Article 67 — Exchange of Information

**Chapter:** 7 — Cooperation and Consistency

## Summary
Article 67 is a brief enabling provision that authorises the Commission to adopt implementing acts specifying the arrangements for electronic exchange of information between supervisory authorities, and between supervisory authorities and the EDPB. This includes, in particular, the standardised format referred to in Article 64. Those implementing acts must be adopted using the examination procedure under Article 93(2). Article 67 provides the formal legal basis for the Commission to fill in the technical and procedural details of inter-SA and SA-Board communications, ensuring that cooperation under Chapter 7 is operationally uniform across the Union.

## Key Obligations
- The **Commission may adopt implementing acts** to specify the format and procedures for electronic information exchange under Chapter 7.
- Implementing acts must follow the **examination procedure** (Article 93(2)).
- All electronic communications covered by Chapter 7 should use the **standardised format** once specified.

## Key Rights / Definitions
- **Implementing acts**: Commission acts of general application adopted under Article 291 TFEU; used here to set technical standards for SA–Board communications.
- **Standardised format**: The electronic communication template referenced in Article 64 and throughout Chapter 7; intended to ensure speed, consistency, and interoperability between national SAs and the EDPB.

## Applies To
The Commission, supervisory authorities, the European Data Protection Board.

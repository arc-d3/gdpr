# Article 69 — Independence

**Chapter:** 7 — Cooperation and Consistency

## Summary
Article 69 guarantees the independence of the European Data Protection Board. When performing its tasks or exercising its powers under Articles 70 (tasks) and 71 (reports), the Board must act completely independently. It may neither seek nor accept instructions from any person or body outside the Board. The only exception concerns requests from the Commission under Article 70(1) and (2), which the Board may respond to; however, even in those cases the Board acts on its own judgment and is not bound to follow the Commission's preferred outcome. This independence mirrors the independence required of national SAs under Article 52, scaling it to the supranational level.

## Key Obligations
- The EDPB must act **independently** when performing its tasks and exercising its powers (Articles 70–71).
- The Board may **neither seek nor take instructions** from any outside body.
- Commission requests under Article 70(1)–(2) are permitted; responding to them does **not** compromise independence.

## Key Rights / Definitions
- **Independence (Board)**: Structural and operational freedom from government, industry, or political influence when the EDPB performs its functions; analogous to national SA independence under Article 52.

## Applies To
The European Data Protection Board, all external parties (governments, institutions, private entities).

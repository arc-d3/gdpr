# Article 62 — Joint Operations of Supervisory Authorities

**Chapter:** 7 — Cooperation and Consistency

## Summary
Article 62 enables supervisory authorities to conduct joint operations — including joint investigations and joint enforcement measures — involving staff from SAs of different Member States. Where a controller or processor has establishments in multiple Member States, or significantly affects data subjects in more than one Member State, the competent SA under Article 56 must invite the SAs of all affected states to participate. Visiting (seconding) staff may exercise investigative powers under the host state's law, but only under the guidance and in the presence of host SA staff. Liability for damage caused by seconding staff rests with the host Member State, which may then seek full reimbursement from the seconding state. If an SA fails to respond to a joint operation invitation within one month, other SAs may adopt provisional measures and the urgency procedure is presumed to be triggered.

## Key Obligations
- SAs shall **conduct joint operations** where appropriate.
- Where multi-state impact is present, the lead SA **must invite** other relevant SAs to participate.
- Invitations must be responded to **without delay**.
- Seconding staff must operate **under the guidance of and in the presence of host SA staff**.
- Investigative powers must be exercised in accordance with **host or seconding Member State law** (as applicable).
- The **host Member State bears liability** for damage caused by seconding staff; it is then reimbursed by the seconding Member State.
- Failure to comply with the invitation obligation within **one month** entitles other SAs to adopt provisional measures and triggers the Article 66 urgency procedure.

## Key Rights / Definitions
- **Joint operations**: Joint investigations and enforcement measures conducted by staff of SAs from more than one Member State simultaneously.
- **Seconding supervisory authority**: The SA whose members or staff are invited to operate on the territory of another Member State's SA.
- **Host supervisory authority**: The SA in whose Member State the joint operation takes place and whose staff lead and supervise the operation.

## Applies To
Supervisory authorities, Member States.

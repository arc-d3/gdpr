# Article 71 — Reports

**Chapter:** 7 — Cooperation and Consistency

## Summary
Article 71 requires the EDPB to prepare an annual report on the protection of natural persons with regard to data processing in the Union and, where relevant, in third countries and international organisations. The report must be made public and transmitted to the European Parliament, the Council, and the Commission. It must include a review of the practical application of the Board's guidelines, recommendations, and best practices (Article 70(1)(l)), as well as a review of its binding decisions under Article 65. This reporting obligation ensures democratic accountability of the EDPB and provides a comprehensive annual overview of data protection developments across the EU.

## Key Obligations
- Prepare and publish an **annual report** covering data protection in the Union (and third countries where relevant).
- Transmit the report to the **European Parliament, Council, and Commission**.
- Include a **review of guidelines, recommendations, and best practices** (Article 70(1)(l)).
- Include a **review of binding decisions** adopted under Article 65.

## Key Rights / Definitions
- **Annual report**: The EDPB's primary public accountability document; summarises data protection developments, enforcement trends, and the Board's own output for the reporting year.

## Applies To
The European Data Protection Board.

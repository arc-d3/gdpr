# Art. 13 GDPR – Information to Be Provided Where Personal Data Are Collected from the Data Subject

## Summary

Article 13 applies whenever a controller **collects personal data directly from the data subject**. All required information must be provided **at the time of collection** — not after.

### Paragraph 1 — Mandatory Information (always required)

| # | Element |
|---|---------|
| a | Identity and contact details of the controller (and representative, if applicable) |
| b | Contact details of the DPO (if applicable) |
| c | Purposes of processing and legal basis |
| d | Legitimate interests pursued (if legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f)) |
| e | Recipients or categories of recipients |
| f | Third-country transfers: adequacy decision status, or appropriate safeguards reference |

### Paragraph 2 — Further Information (for fair and transparent processing)

| # | Element |
|---|---------|
| a | Retention period or criteria to determine it |
| b | Existence of rights: access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, portability |
| c | Right to withdraw consent at any time (if legal basis is consent) |
| d | Right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority |
| e | Whether provision of data is statutory/contractual/required for contract; consequences of not providing |
| f | Existence of automated decision-making including profiling (Art. 22(1)(4)), logic, significance, consequences |

### Paragraph 3 — Further Processing
If the controller intends to use data for a **purpose other than originally collected**, it must provide notice of the new purpose and any additional Art. 2 information **before** that further processing begins.

### Paragraph 4 — Exception
Paragraphs 1–3 do not apply where the **data subject already has the information**.

## Todo List

- [ ] Audit all data collection touchpoints (forms, sign-ups, contracts, cookies, etc.) and confirm a compliant Art. 13 privacy notice is presented **at time of collection**.
- [ ] Verify privacy notices include all six paragraph 1 elements; assign a responsible owner per data flow.
- [ ] Verify privacy notices include all six paragraph 2 elements, including retention periods and automated decision-making disclosures.
- [ ] Where processing is based on consent, confirm the notice explicitly states the right to withdraw consent at any time.
- [ ] Where data provision is mandatory (statutory/contractual), document and disclose this and the consequences of refusal.
- [ ] Implement a **further-purpose notification process**: flag when secondary uses are planned and trigger a fresh notice before processing begins.
- [ ] Review notice accessibility: plain language, readable format, appropriate for all audience segments (including children where applicable).
- [ ] Maintain a version-controlled register of all Art. 13 notices with effective dates.
- [ ] Conduct periodic reviews (at least annually) to ensure notices remain accurate and up to date.

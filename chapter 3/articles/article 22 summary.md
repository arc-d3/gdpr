# Art. 22 GDPR – Automated Individual Decision-Making, Including Profiling

## Summary

Article 22 protects data subjects from decisions that are made **exclusively by automated means** and produce **legal or similarly significant effects** on them.

### Paragraph 1 — General Prohibition
Data subjects have the right **not to be subject to** a decision based **solely on automated processing** — including profiling — that:
- produces **legal effects** concerning them; or
- **similarly significantly affects** them (e.g., automatic denial of credit, insurance, employment).

### Paragraph 2 — Exceptions
The prohibition does not apply if the decision:

| Exception | Condition |
|-----------|-----------|
| (a) **Contract necessity** | The decision is necessary for entering into or performing a contract between the data subject and the controller. |
| (b) **Legal authorisation** | The decision is authorised by Union or Member State law with suitable safeguards. |
| (c) **Explicit consent** | The decision is based on the data subject's explicit consent. |

### Paragraph 3 — Safeguards for Exceptions (a) and (c)
Where processing falls under exception (a) or (c), the controller must implement **at least**:
- The right to obtain **human intervention** by the controller;
- The right to **express the data subject's point of view**;
- The right to **contest the decision**.

### Paragraph 4 — Special Category Data
Automated decisions under paragraph 2 must **not be based on special-category data** (Art. 9(1)) unless:
- Art. 9(2)(a) applies (explicit consent); or
- Art. 9(2)(g) applies (substantial public interest),
and suitable safeguards are in place.

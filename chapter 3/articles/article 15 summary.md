# Art. 15 GDPR – Right of Access by the Data Subject

## Summary

Article 15 grants data subjects the right to **know whether their data is being processed** and, if so, to obtain a **copy** of that data together with supplementary information.

### Paragraph 1 — Scope of Access
The data subject may obtain confirmation of processing and access to:

| # | Element to disclose |
|---|---------------------|
| a | Purposes of the processing |
| b | Categories of personal data |
| c | Recipients or categories of recipients (including in third countries) |
| d | Envisaged retention period or criteria to determine it |
| e | Existence of rights to rectification, erasure, restriction, and objection |
| f | Right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority |
| g | Source of data (if not collected from the data subject) |
| h | Existence of automated decision-making including profiling (logic, significance, consequences) |

### Paragraph 2 — Third-Country Transfers
Where data are transferred to a third country or international organisation, the data subject is entitled to information about the **appropriate safeguards** in place (Art. 46).

### Paragraphs 3–4 — Copy of Data
- The controller must provide **one free copy** of the personal data undergoing processing.
- **Additional copies** may attract a reasonable fee based on administrative costs.
- Requests made **electronically** should be responded to electronically (unless the data subject requests otherwise).
- The right to a copy must **not adversely affect** the rights and freedoms of others (e.g., trade secrets, third-party data).

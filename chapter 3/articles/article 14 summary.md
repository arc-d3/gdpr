# Art. 14 GDPR – Information to Be Provided Where Personal Data Have Not Been Obtained from the Data Subject

## Summary

Article 14 mirrors Art. 13 but applies when the controller obtains personal data from a **source other than the data subject** (e.g., data brokers, public registries, other organisations). Because the data subject was not present at collection, additional disclosure obligations and delivery deadlines apply.

### Paragraph 1 — Mandatory Information (same as Art. 13(1), plus the data category)

| # | Element |
|---|---------|
| a | Identity and contact details of the controller (and representative) |
| b | Contact details of the DPO (if applicable) |
| c | Purposes and legal basis for processing |
| d | **Categories of personal data concerned** *(extra vs. Art. 13)* |
| e | Recipients or categories of recipients |
| f | Third-country transfers: adequacy decision or safeguards reference |

### Paragraph 2 — Further Information

| # | Element |
|---|---------|
| a | Retention period or criteria |
| b | Legitimate interests (if legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f)) |
| c | Existence of rights: access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, portability |
| d | Right to withdraw consent (if consent is the legal basis) |
| e | Right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority |
| f | **Source of the data** (and whether from publicly accessible sources) *(extra vs. Art. 13)* |
| g | Existence of automated decision-making including profiling (logic, significance, consequences) |

### Paragraph 3 — Delivery Deadlines
The controller must provide the information:
- **(a)** Within a **reasonable period** (at most **one month**) after obtaining the data; or
- **(b)** At the **time of first communication** with the data subject (if data will be used for communication); or
- **(c)** At the **time of first disclosure** to another recipient (whichever is earliest).

### Paragraphs 4–5 — Further Processing & Exceptions
Same further-purpose notification obligation as Art. 13(3). Exceptions apply where:
- The data subject already has the information.
- Provision is **impossible or involves disproportionate effort** (archiving/research/statistics under Art. 89).
- Obtaining/disclosure is expressly laid down by Union/Member State law with appropriate safeguards.
- Data must remain **confidential** under a professional secrecy obligation.

# Art. 23 GDPR – Restrictions

## Summary

Article 23 is the **legislative restriction mechanism**: it permits Union or Member State law to **limit the scope of data subject rights (Art. 12–22)** and Art. 5 obligations, provided the restriction is a **necessary and proportionate** measure in a democratic society.

### Paragraph 1 — Permitted Grounds for Restriction

| # | Ground |
|---|--------|
| (a) | **National security** |
| (b) | **Defence** |
| (c) | **Public security** |
| (d) | Prevention, investigation, detection, or prosecution of **criminal offences** or execution of criminal penalties; safeguarding against threats to public security |
| (e) | Other important objectives of **general public interest** (e.g., economic/financial interests, monetary/budgetary/taxation matters, public health, social security) |
| (f) | Protection of **judicial independence** and judicial proceedings |
| (g) | Prevention, investigation, detection, and prosecution of breaches of **ethics for regulated professions** |
| (h) | **Monitoring, inspection, or regulatory function** connected to the exercise of official authority in cases (a)–(e) and (g) |
| (i) | Protection of the **data subject** or the rights and freedoms of others |
| (j) | Enforcement of **civil law claims** |

### Paragraph 2 — Minimum Content of Any Restriction Measure
Any legislative restriction measure must contain **specific provisions** covering at least, where relevant:

| # | Required element |
|---|-----------------|
| (a) | Purposes of processing or categories of processing |
| (b) | Categories of personal data |
| (c) | Scope of the restrictions introduced |
| (d) | Safeguards to prevent abuse or unlawful access or transfer |
| (e) | Specification of the controller or categories of controllers |
| (f) | Storage periods and applicable safeguards |
| (g) | Risks to the rights and freedoms of data subjects |
| (h) | Right of data subjects to be informed about the restriction (unless prejudicial to the restriction's purpose) |

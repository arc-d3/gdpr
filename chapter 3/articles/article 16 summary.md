# Art. 16 GDPR – Right to Rectification

## Summary

Article 16 gives data subjects the right to have **inaccurate or incomplete personal data corrected** without undue delay.

| Scenario | Controller's obligation |
|----------|------------------------|
| Data is **inaccurate** | Rectify (correct) the erroneous data without undue delay. |
| Data is **incomplete** | Complete the data, including by means of a supplementary statement provided by the data subject. |

The article is brief but has important downstream effects:
- Any rectification carried out must be **communicated to all recipients** of the data under **Art. 19**.
- The standard Art. 12 response deadline of **one month** applies.
- Member States or Union law may restrict the right under **Art. 23** for specific public-interest purposes.

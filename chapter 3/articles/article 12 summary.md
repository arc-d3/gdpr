# Art. 12 GDPR – Transparent Information, Communication and Modalities for the Exercise of the Rights of the Data Subject

## Summary

Article 12 sets out the **procedural rules** that govern how a controller must communicate with data subjects when providing information under Art. 13–14 and when handling rights requests under Art. 15–22 and Art. 34.

| Requirement | Detail |
|-------------|--------|
| **Form of communication** | Concise, transparent, intelligible, easily accessible; clear and plain language; in writing or electronic means; orally on request (with identity verification). |
| **Facilitation of rights** | Controller must actively facilitate the exercise of Art. 15–22 rights; may not refuse unless unable to identify the data subject (Art. 11). |
| **Response deadline** | Without undue delay and **within one month** of receipt of request. |
| **Extension** | Up to **two further months** for complex/numerous requests; data subject must be notified within the first month with reasons. |
| **No action** | If no action is taken, the controller must inform the data subject within one month of reasons and right to complain to a supervisory authority / seek judicial remedy. |
| **Free of charge** | All information and responses are free unless requests are **manifestly unfounded or excessive** (especially repetitive). |
| **Excessive requests** | Controller may charge a reasonable fee or refuse; controller bears burden of proving the request is manifestly unfounded/excessive. |
| **Identity verification** | Controller may request additional information to confirm identity where reasonable doubts exist. |
| **Standardised icons** | Art. 13/14 information may be supplemented with machine-readable standardised icons (Commission empowered to adopt delegated acts). |

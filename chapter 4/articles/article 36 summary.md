# Art. 36 GDPR – Prior Consultation

## Summary

Article 36 requires controllers to **consult the supervisory authority** before commencing processing where a DPIA (Art. 35) indicates a **residual high risk** that cannot be adequately mitigated. The supervisory authority must respond within 8 weeks (extendable by 6 weeks for complex cases).

### Prior Consultation Trigger

| Condition | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Trigger** | DPIA indicates the processing would result in a high risk in the absence of measures taken by the controller to mitigate the risk — i.e. residual high risk remains after mitigation. |

### Supervisory Authority Response

| Aspect | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| **Timeline** | Up to **8 weeks** from receipt of the consultation request. |
| **Extension** | May be extended by **6 weeks** for complex processing; controller must be informed within 1 month with reasons. |
| **Suspension** | Periods may be suspended while the supervisory authority seeks further information. |
| **Powers** | The supervisory authority may provide written advice and exercise any of its Art. 58 powers (including prohibition). |

### Information to Provide to the Supervisory Authority (Art. 36(3))

| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **(a) Responsibilities** | Respective responsibilities of controller, joint controllers, and processors involved. |
| **(b) Purposes and means** | The intended purposes and means of processing. |
| **(c) Measures and safeguards** | Measures and safeguards to protect data subjects. |
| **(d) DPO contact** | Where applicable, the DPO's contact details. |
| **(e) DPIA** | The full DPIA from Art. 35. |
| **(f) Other information** | Any additional information requested by the supervisory authority. |

### Additional Rules

| Rule | Detail |
|------|--------|
| **Legislation** | Member States must consult the supervisory authority when preparing legislation relating to processing. |
| **Public interest** | Member State law may require prior authorisation for certain public-interest processing. |

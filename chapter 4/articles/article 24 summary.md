# Art. 24 GDPR – Responsibility of the Controller

## Summary

Article 24 establishes the **accountability principle** in operational terms. Controllers must implement appropriate technical and organisational measures (TOMs) to both ensure and be able to demonstrate that their processing complies with the GDPR. The measures must be proportionate to the nature, scope, context, purposes and risks of the processing, and must be reviewed and updated as circumstances change.

| Requirement | Detail |
|-------------|--------|
| **TOMs** | Appropriate technical and organisational measures to ensure and demonstrate GDPR compliance. |
| **Risk calibration** | Measures must account for nature, scope, context, purposes of processing and the risk to rights and freedoms. |
| **Data protection policy** | Where proportionate, formal data protection policies must be implemented. |
| **Review obligation** | Measures must be reviewed and updated where necessary. |
| **Compliance evidence** | Adherence to approved codes of conduct (Art. 40) or certification mechanisms (Art. 42) may be used as evidence of compliance. |

## Todo List

- [ ] Document and formalise the organisation's **technical and organisational measures (TOMs)** in a policy or register, covering all processing activities.
- [ ] Calibrate TOMs by conducting a **risk inventory** that maps processing activities to their nature, scope, context, purposes and likelihood/severity of risks.
- [ ] Adopt or update a **data protection policy** appropriate to the scale and nature of processing operations.
- [ ] Schedule **periodic reviews** of TOMs (at minimum annually, and following any significant change to processing activities).
- [ ] Maintain an **accountability record** (e.g. internal audit logs, policy version history) to demonstrate compliance to supervisory authorities upon request.
- [ ] Assess whether adherence to an **approved code of conduct** (Art. 40) or obtaining **certification** (Art. 42) would strengthen the demonstrability of compliance.
- [ ] Ensure senior management (Board/C-suite) formally **approves and owns** the data protection policy.
- [ ] Train all staff who carry out processing on the TOMs in place and their personal obligations under the controller's framework.

# Art. 31 GDPR – Cooperation with the Supervisory Authority

## Summary

Article 31 is a concise but important obligation: the controller, the processor, and, where applicable, their representatives must **cooperate with the supervisory authority on request** in the performance of its tasks.

| Requirement | Detail |
|-------------|--------|
| **Duty to cooperate** | Cooperation is mandatory; it is not discretionary. |
| **Who must cooperate** | The controller and processor, and (where applicable) their Art. 27 representatives. |
| **When** | On request from the supervisory authority in the performance of its tasks (which include investigations, audits, handling complaints, and enforcement). |

Non-cooperation can constitute a breach of GDPR in its own right and may attract administrative fines under Art. 83(4) (up to €10 million or 2% of global annual turnover).

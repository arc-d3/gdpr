# Art. 43 GDPR – Certification Bodies

## Summary

Article 43 sets out the requirements for **accreditation of certification bodies** that issue GDPR certifications under Art. 42. Certification bodies must be accredited by the supervisory authority or a national accreditation body and must demonstrate independence, expertise, and robust procedures.

### Accreditation Pathways

| Pathway | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **(a) Supervisory authority accreditation** | The competent supervisory authority (Art. 55 or 56) accredits the certification body. |
| **(b) National accreditation body** | The national accreditation body designated under Regulation (EC) No 765/2008, in accordance with EN-ISO/IEC 17065/2012, with additional requirements set by the supervisory authority. |

### Requirements for Accreditation (Art. 43(2))

| Requirement | Detail |
|-------------|--------|
| **(a) Independence and expertise** | Demonstrated independence and expertise in the certification subject matter. |
| **(b) Respect Art. 42(5) criteria** | Must commit to and comply with the certification criteria approved by the supervisory authority or Board. |
| **(c) Issuance, review, and withdrawal procedures** | Procedures for issuing, periodically reviewing, and withdrawing certifications. |
| **(d) Complaint procedures** | Transparent procedures and structures for handling complaints about certification infringements; transparent to data subjects and public. |
| **(e) No conflict of interests** | Tasks and duties must not result in a conflict of interests. |

### Key Operational Rules

| Rule | Detail |
|------|--------|
| **Accreditation duration** | Maximum **5 years**; renewable under the same conditions. |
| **Reasons for certification decisions** | Certification bodies must provide the supervisory authority with reasons for granting or withdrawing certification. |
| **Revocation of accreditation** | Supervisory authority or national accreditation body must revoke accreditation if conditions cease to be met or if actions infringe GDPR. |
| **Responsibility** | Certification body is responsible for the proper assessment; controller/processor retains full GDPR compliance responsibility. |

### Commission Powers

| Power | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| **Delegated acts** | Commission may adopt delegated acts (Art. 92) specifying requirements for certification mechanisms. |
| **Implementing acts** | Commission may adopt implementing acts (Art. 93(2)) laying down technical standards for certification mechanisms, seals, and marks. |

## Todo List

- [ ] When selecting a certification body (Art. 42), verify it holds **valid accreditation** from the supervisory authority or a recognised national accreditation body.
- [ ] Confirm the certification body has **transparent complaint procedures** accessible to data subjects and the public.
- [ ] Verify the certification body has **no conflict of interests** in relation to the organisation's processing activities.
- [ ] Ensure the certification body commits to **providing reasons** to the supervisory authority if certification is withdrawn.
- [ ] Calendar the **5-year accreditation renewal** for any certification bodies used, and reassess at renewal.
- [ ] Monitor the **supervisory authority's published requirements** for certification body accreditation and ensure the chosen body remains compliant.
- [ ] If the organisation is seeking to become a certification body, engage the supervisory authority early to understand accreditation requirements and submit a compliant application.
- [ ] Track **Commission delegated and implementing acts** on certification standards (Art. 43(8)–(9)) and ensure any used certification mechanisms comply.

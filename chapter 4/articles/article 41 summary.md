# Art. 41 GDPR – Monitoring of Approved Codes of Conduct

## Summary

Article 41 provides for the **accreditation of independent monitoring bodies** to oversee compliance with approved codes of conduct (Art. 40). These bodies operate alongside, not in place of, supervisory authorities.

| Aspect | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| **Monitoring entity** | A body accredited by the competent supervisory authority, with appropriate expertise in the code's subject matter. |
| **Not the supervisory authority** | The supervisory authority's own tasks and powers (Art. 57–58) are unaffected; the monitoring body supplements, not replaces, supervisory authority oversight. |
| **Does not apply to** | Processing carried out by public authorities and bodies. |

### Accreditation Requirements (Art. 41(2))

| Requirement | Detail |
|-------------|--------|
| **(a) Independence and expertise** | Must demonstrate independence and expertise in the code's subject matter to the supervisory authority's satisfaction. |
| **(b) Assessment and monitoring procedures** | Procedures for assessing controller/processor eligibility to apply the code, monitoring ongoing compliance, and periodically reviewing operation. |
| **(c) Complaint procedures** | Transparent procedures and structures for handling complaints about code infringements. |
| **(d) No conflict of interests** | Tasks and duties must not result in a conflict of interests. |

### Powers and Obligations of the Monitoring Body

| Power/Obligation | Detail |
|-----------------|--------|
| **Action on infringement** | Must take appropriate action, including **suspension or exclusion** from the code, subject to appropriate safeguards. |
| **Reporting** | Must inform the competent supervisory authority of actions taken and reasons. |
| **Revocation** | Supervisory authority may revoke accreditation if requirements cease to be met or if the body's actions infringe GDPR. |

## Todo List

- [ ] Where adhering to an approved code, verify the **accredited monitoring body** is in place and understand how to engage with it for compliance assessments.
- [ ] Ensure complaints about code compliance can be **submitted to the monitoring body** and that procedures are transparent to data subjects.
- [ ] If the organisation is itself seeking accreditation as a monitoring body, demonstrate **independence, expertise, and conflict-of-interest controls** to the supervisory authority.
- [ ] If acting as a monitoring body, implement **assessment and periodic review procedures** covering all controllers/processors adhering to the code.
- [ ] Maintain records of **actions taken against non-compliant adherents** and reports submitted to the supervisory authority.
- [ ] Note that public authorities are **exempt from Art. 41** monitoring body oversight; supervisory authority oversight applies directly instead.

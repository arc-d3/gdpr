# Art. 35 GDPR – Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA)

## Summary

Article 35 requires controllers to conduct a **Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA)** before carrying out processing that is likely to result in a **high risk** to the rights and freedoms of natural persons. The DPO must be consulted. Supervisory authorities publish lists of processing types that are required to undergo a DPIA.

### When a DPIA Is Mandatory

| Trigger | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Systematic profiling** | Systematic and extensive evaluation of personal aspects (including profiling) based on automated processing that produces legal or similarly significant effects. |
| **Large-scale special categories** | Processing on a large scale of special category data (Art. 9(1)) or criminal conviction/offence data (Art. 10). |
| **Large-scale public monitoring** | Systematic monitoring of a publicly accessible area on a large scale. |
| **Supervisory authority lists** | Any processing type listed by the supervisory authority as requiring a DPIA. |

### Mandatory DPIA Content (Art. 35(7))

| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **(a) Description** | Systematic description of processing operations and purposes, including legitimate interests (if applicable). |
| **(b) Necessity and proportionality** | Assessment of the necessity and proportionality of the processing in relation to its purposes. |
| **(c) Risk assessment** | Assessment of the risks to the rights and freedoms of data subjects. |
| **(d) Mitigating measures** | Safeguards, security measures, and mechanisms to address risks and demonstrate compliance. |

### Additional Rules

| Rule | Detail |
|------|--------|
| **DPO consultation** | Controller must seek the DPO's advice when conducting a DPIA. |
| **Data subject views** | Controller should consider seeking views of data subjects or their representatives (where appropriate). |
| **Code of conduct** | Adherence to an approved code of conduct is taken into account when assessing impact. |
| **Single DPIA** | One DPIA may cover a set of similar processing operations with similar high risks. |
| **Review obligation** | Controller must carry out a review when there is a change in the risk represented by the processing. |
| **Legal basis exemption** | If a law regulating the specific processing already included a general impact assessment, the DPIA requirement may not apply. |

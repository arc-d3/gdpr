# Art. 29 GDPR – Processing Under the Authority of the Controller or Processor

## Summary

Article 29 is a short but foundational rule: **processors and anyone acting under the authority of the controller or processor** (e.g. employees, contractors, agents) may process personal data **only on the documented instructions** of the controller. An exception exists where Union or Member State law requires otherwise.

| Requirement | Detail |
|-------------|--------|
| **Scope** | Applies to the processor and any person acting under the authority of the controller or processor who has access to personal data. |
| **Instruction-only processing** | Personal data must not be processed except on instructions from the controller. |
| **Legal exception** | Processing outside instructions is permitted if required by Union or Member State law (processor must inform controller unless prohibited). |

This article reinforces the DPA requirement in Art. 28(3)(a) and the security-of-processing obligations in Art. 32(4) that personnel may only process on the controller's instructions.

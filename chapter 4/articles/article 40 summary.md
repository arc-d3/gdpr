# Art. 40 GDPR – Codes of Conduct

## Summary

Article 40 encourages the development of **sector-specific codes of conduct** that specify how GDPR applies in particular processing contexts. Codes are developed by industry associations, approved by supervisory authorities, and may achieve Union-wide validity via a Commission implementing act.

### Who Develops Codes

| Party | Role |
|-------|------|
| **Associations and representative bodies** | Prepare, amend, or extend codes on behalf of controllers or processors in specific sectors. |
| **Member States, supervisory authorities, Board, Commission** | Encourage the drawing up of codes; account for needs of SMEs. |

### Topics Codes May Cover (Non-exhaustive)

| Topic | Art. 40(2) ref. |
|-------|----------------|
| Fair and transparent processing | (a) |
| Legitimate interests in specific contexts | (b) |
| Data collection practices | (c) |
| Pseudonymisation of personal data | (d) |
| Information to the public and data subjects | (e) |
| Exercise of data subject rights | (f) |
| Children's data and parental consent | (g) |
| Privacy by design/default and security (Art. 24–25, 32) | (h) |
| Breach notification (Art. 33–34) | (i) |
| Transfers to third countries | (j) |
| Out-of-court dispute resolution | (k) |

### Approval Process

| Stage | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| **Submit to supervisory authority** | The draft code is submitted to the competent supervisory authority (Art. 55) for opinion and approval. |
| **Single-country code** | If approved and limited to one Member State: supervisory authority registers and publishes it. |
| **Multi-country code** | Submitted via the consistency mechanism (Art. 63) to the Board for an opinion, then to the Commission. |
| **Union-wide validity** | Commission may adopt an implementing act granting the code general validity across the EU (Art. 93(2)). |

### Non-EU Controllers
Controllers/processors outside the EU may adhere to an approved code (with binding commitments) as a transfer safeguard under Art. 46(2)(e).

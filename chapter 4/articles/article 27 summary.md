# Art. 27 GDPR – Representatives of Controllers or Processors Not Established in the Union

## Summary

Article 27 requires controllers and processors outside the EU that are subject to GDPR under Art. 3(2) (targeting EU data subjects) to **designate a representative in writing** in a relevant EU Member State. The representative acts as the contact for supervisory authorities and data subjects.

| Requirement | Detail |
|-------------|--------|
| **Trigger** | Art. 3(2) applies: non-EU entity offering goods/services to EU data subjects, or monitoring their behaviour. |
| **Designation** | Must be in writing. |
| **Location** | The representative must be established in one of the Member States where data subjects whose data are being processed are located. |
| **Role** | Addressable by supervisory authorities and data subjects on all issues relating to processing for GDPR compliance. |
| **No prejudice** | Designating a representative does not prevent legal action against the controller or processor directly. |
| **Exceptions** | (a) Occasional processing, no large-scale special category or criminal data, unlikely to risk rights/freedoms; OR (b) public authority or body. |

# Art. 39 GDPR – Tasks of the Data Protection Officer

## Summary

Article 39 defines the **minimum tasks** that the DPO must carry out. The DPO must prioritise tasks based on the risk associated with processing operations.

### Minimum DPO Tasks

| Task | Detail |
|------|--------|
| **(a) Inform and advise** | Inform and advise the controller, processor and processing staff of their obligations under GDPR and other data protection provisions. |
| **(b) Monitor compliance** | Monitor compliance with GDPR, other Union/Member State provisions, and internal data protection policies — including assigning responsibilities, raising awareness, training staff, and conducting related audits. |
| **(c) DPIA advice and monitoring** | Provide advice on DPIAs (Art. 35) and monitor their performance. |
| **(d) Cooperate with supervisory authority** | Cooperate with the supervisory authority in the performance of its tasks. |
| **(e) Contact point for supervisory authority** | Act as the contact point for the supervisory authority on all processing issues, including prior consultation (Art. 36). |
| **Risk-based prioritisation** | The DPO must have due regard to the risk associated with processing when prioritising tasks, taking into account nature, scope, context, and purposes. |

## Todo List

- [ ] Develop a **DPO work plan** that formally documents how each of the five Art. 39 tasks will be carried out, with allocated time and resources.
- [ ] Implement a **GDPR training and awareness programme** managed or overseen by the DPO, covering all staff with data processing responsibilities.
- [ ] Schedule **regular compliance audits** of key processing activities, policies, and procedures, with findings reported to senior management.
- [ ] Formally embed the DPO in the **DPIA process** (Art. 35): DPO must be consulted at initiation, during assessment, and before finalisation.
- [ ] Maintain a **log of DPO advisory opinions** issued on DPIAs, new processing activities, contracts, and other matters.
- [ ] Ensure the DPO is registered as the **contact point with the supervisory authority** and that the supervisory authority has up-to-date contact details.
- [ ] Develop a **risk-based prioritisation framework** for the DPO's activities, focusing time on highest-risk processing activities.
- [ ] Conduct an annual review of the DPO's tasks against Art. 39 to confirm all minimum tasks are being fulfilled.

# Art. 37 GDPR – Designation of the Data Protection Officer

## Summary

Article 37 sets out the circumstances in which a **Data Protection Officer (DPO)** must be designated, the qualifications required, and the administrative requirements of the designation.

### When a DPO Is Mandatory

| Scenario | Detail |
|----------|--------|
| **(a) Public authority or body** | Processing is carried out by a public authority or body (except courts in their judicial capacity). |
| **(b) Large-scale systematic monitoring** | Core activities require regular and systematic monitoring of data subjects on a large scale. |
| **(c) Large-scale special categories** | Core activities consist of large-scale processing of special category data (Art. 9) or criminal conviction/offence data (Art. 10). |

### Flexibility Provisions

| Provision | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Group DPO** | A group of undertakings may appoint a single DPO, provided the DPO is easily accessible from each establishment. |
| **Shared public authority DPO** | A single DPO may cover several public authorities/bodies, considering organisational structure and size. |
| **Voluntary designation** | Controllers/processors not meeting mandatory criteria may voluntarily appoint a DPO. Union or Member State law may also require designation. |

### Qualification and Appointment Rules

| Rule | Detail |
|------|--------|
| **Qualifications** | Professional qualities plus expert knowledge of data protection law and practices, and ability to fulfil Art. 39 tasks. |
| **Employment form** | Staff member or service contract (external DPO). |
| **Publication** | Controller/processor must publish the DPO's contact details. |
| **Notification** | DPO contact details must be communicated to the supervisory authority. |

- [ ] Conduct a **DPO designation assessment**: determine whether mandatory criteria (a), (b), or (c) apply to the organisation.
- [ ] If mandatory, **designate a DPO** with appropriate professional qualifications and expert knowledge of data protection law (consider internal vs. external appointment).

- [ ] If using a single DPO for a group of undertakings, confirm the DPO is **easily accessible** from each establishment (Art. 37(2)).
- [ ] **Publish the DPO's contact details** on the organisation's website and in privacy notices.
- [ ] **Notify the DPO's contact details** to the relevant supervisory authority (or authorities).
- [ ] Ensure the DPO's **service contract or employment contract** clearly defines the DPO role, tasks, and independence protections.
- [ ] Reassess whether mandatory designation criteria continue to be met whenever the organisation's processing activities change significantly.

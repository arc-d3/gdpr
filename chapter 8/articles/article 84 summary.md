# Article 84 — Penalties

**Chapter:** 8 — Remedies, Liability and Penalties

## Summary
Article 84 requires Member States to establish national rules on penalties for GDPR infringements that are not subject to administrative fines under Article 83. These national penalties must be effective, proportionate, and dissuasive, and Member States must take all measures necessary to ensure they are applied. Each Member State must notify the Commission of the relevant national legal provisions by 25 May 2018 and promptly notify any subsequent amendments. The article fills enforcement gaps left by Article 83 by mandating national-level sanctions for any infringement outside Article 83's scope.

## Key Obligations
- **Member States**: Must lay down national penalty rules for non-Article 83 infringements; must ensure those penalties are effective, proportionate, and dissuasive; must implement them in practice; must notify the Commission by 25 May 2018 and of any amendments.

## Key Rights / Definitions
- **National penalties**: Sanctions established by Member State law for GDPR infringements not covered by Article 83 — may include criminal sanctions, civil penalties, or administrative measures beyond those in Article 83.
- **Effective, proportionate, dissuasive**: The same three mandatory characteristics required for all GDPR penalties, mirroring the standard set by Article 83.

## Applies To
Member States, controllers, processors, any entity subject to GDPR obligations.

## Cross-References
- Article 83 (administrative fines — the primary penalty mechanism; Article 84 covers infringements outside Article 83's scope)

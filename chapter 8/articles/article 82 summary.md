# Article 82 — Right to compensation and liability

**Chapter:** 8 — Remedies, Liability and Penalties

## Summary
Article 82 establishes that any person who suffers material or non-material damage from a GDPR infringement has the right to receive compensation from the responsible controller or processor. Controllers are liable for all processing they are involved in that infringes the GDPR. Processors are liable only where they have failed to comply with obligations specifically directed to processors, or where they acted outside or contrary to the controller's lawful instructions. A controller or processor is exempt from liability if it proves it bears no responsibility for the event that caused the damage. Where multiple parties are co-responsible, each is liable for the entire damage (joint and several liability), but internal recourse between them is possible to apportion responsibility. Compensation proceedings are brought before the courts designated by Article 79(2).

## Key Obligations
- **Controller**: Liable for damage caused by any infringing processing it is involved in unless it proves it bears no responsibility for the causing event.
- **Processor**: Liable only where it failed to comply with processor-specific GDPR obligations or acted outside/contrary to lawful controller instructions.
- **Co-responsible parties**: Each fully liable to the claimant; may seek proportionate recourse from other co-responsible controllers/processors after paying full compensation.

## Key Rights / Definitions
- **Right to compensation**: Covers both material damage (financial loss) and non-material damage (distress, reputational harm, etc.) caused by a GDPR infringement.
- **Exemption from liability**: Available where a party proves it is not in any way responsible for the event giving rise to the damage — a high bar requiring positive proof.
- **Joint and several liability**: Each co-responsible controller/processor owes full compensation to the claimant; internal contribution claims between them are settled according to their respective shares of responsibility.

## Applies To
Data subjects, controllers, processors.

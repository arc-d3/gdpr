# Article 77 — Right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority

**Chapter:** 8 — Remedies, Liability and Penalties

## Summary
Article 77 guarantees every data subject the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority if they believe the processing of their personal data infringes the GDPR. The data subject may choose the SA of their habitual residence, their place of work, or the place where the alleged infringement occurred. The chosen SA must inform the complainant of the progress and outcome of the complaint, including the possibility of seeking a judicial remedy under Article 78. This right exists without prejudice to any other administrative or judicial remedy.

## Key Obligations
- **Supervisory authority**: Must handle the complaint and keep the complainant informed of both the progress and the outcome, including reference to the judicial remedy available under Article 78.
- **Data subject**: No formal obligations — the right is exercised at the data subject's initiative and is not dependent on prior exhaustion of other remedies.

## Key Rights / Definitions
- **Right to lodge a complaint**: An unconditional right of every data subject to submit a complaint to a competent SA without needing to exhaust other remedies first.
- **Competent SA**: The SA of the Member State of the data subject's habitual residence, place of work, or place of the alleged infringement — the data subject may choose among these.
- **Progress and outcome notification**: The SA must proactively inform the complainant at both stages; silence for three months triggers the judicial remedy under Article 78(2).

## Applies To
Data subjects, supervisory authorities.

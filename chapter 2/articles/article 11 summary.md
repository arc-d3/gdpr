# Art. 11 GDPR – Processing Which Does Not Require Identification

## Summary

Article 11 addresses the situation where a controller **cannot identify** (or no longer needs to identify) a data subject from the data it holds.

- **No obligation to identify** – If the processing purpose does not require identifying a data subject, the controller is not obliged to collect or retain additional information just to comply with the GDPR.
- **Inform the data subject** – Where identification is not possible, the controller must inform the data subject (if feasible).
- **Data subject rights suspended** – Articles 15–20 (access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection) do not apply in such cases **unless** the data subject provides additional information that enables their identification.

This article is particularly relevant for anonymised or pseudonymised datasets, large aggregated datasets, and research contexts.

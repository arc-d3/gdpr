# Art. 7 GDPR – Conditions for Consent

## Summary

Article 7 sets out **four requirements** that must all be met whenever consent is used as the lawful basis for processing.

1. **Demonstrability** – The controller must be able to prove that the data subject has consented (burden of proof on the controller).
2. **Distinguishability** – Where consent is bundled with other terms in a written declaration, the consent request must be clearly separated, presented in plain and intelligible language; any non-compliant part is not binding.
3. **Right to withdraw** – The data subject may withdraw consent at any time, as easily as they gave it; withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of prior processing, but the data subject must be informed of this right *before* giving consent.
4. **Freely given** – Consent is not freely given if performance of a contract (or provision of a service) is made conditional on consenting to processing that is *not necessary* for that contract.

# Art. 8 GDPR – Conditions Applicable to Child's Consent in Relation to Information Society Services

## Summary

Article 8 governs the use of **consent as a lawful basis (Art. 6(1)(a))** when an information society service is offered directly to children.

- **Age threshold (default: 16)** – Processing a child's personal data based on consent is lawful only if the child is at least 16 years old.
- **Below 16 (or lower national threshold, min. 13)** – Processing is lawful only if consent is given or authorised by a person holding parental responsibility.
- **Member State flexibility** – National law may lower the threshold, but it must remain at or above 13 years old.
- **Verification obligation** – The controller must make *reasonable efforts* (taking available technology into account) to verify that parental consent has been obtained for under-age users.
- **Contract law preserved** – This article does not affect general Member State contract law regarding contracts concluded by or on behalf of children.

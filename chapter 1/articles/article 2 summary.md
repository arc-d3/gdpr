# Article 2 – Material Scope

**Chapter:** 1 – General Provisions  
**Link:** https://gdpr-info.eu/art-2-gdpr/

---

## Summary

Article 2 answers the question: *what types of data processing does the GDPR govern?* It applies to:
- **Automated processing** of personal data (e.g. databases, software, cloud systems).
- **Manual processing** of personal data — but only where the data forms part of a structured **filing system**.

It explicitly excludes four categories:
1. Activities outside EU law (e.g. purely national security matters).
2. Member State activities under the Common Foreign & Security Policy (CFSP).
3. **Purely personal or household** activities (e.g. a personal address book).
4. **Law enforcement** processing for criminal investigation/prosecution — covered separately by the Law Enforcement Directive (LED, Directive 2016/680).

EU institutions are not subject to GDPR directly — they fall under Regulation (EC) No 45/2001 (being updated to Regulation 2018/1725).

---

## Legal Text

> **1.** This Regulation applies to the processing of personal data wholly or partly by automated means and to the processing other than by automated means of personal data which form part of a filing system or are intended to form part of a filing system.
>
> **2.** This Regulation does not apply to the processing of personal data:
> - **(a)** in the course of an activity which falls outside the scope of Union law;
> - **(b)** by the Member States when carrying out activities which fall within the scope of Chapter 2 of Title V of the TEU;
> - **(c)** by a natural person in the course of a purely personal or household activity;
> - **(d)** by competent authorities for the purposes of the prevention, investigation, detection or prosecution of criminal offences.
>
> **3.** For processing by Union institutions and bodies, Regulation (EC) No 45/2001 applies.
>
> **4.** This Regulation is without prejudice to Directive 2000/31/EC (e-Commerce Directive).

# Article 91 — Existing Data Protection Rules of Churches and Religious Associations

**Chapter:** 9 — Provisions Relating to Specific Processing Situations

## Summary
Article 91 preserves comprehensive data protection rules applied by churches and religious associations that were already in place when the GDPR entered into force, provided those rules are brought into conformity with the GDPR. Churches and religious associations that apply such rules must be subject to independent supervisory oversight — which may be a body specific to them — so long as that body meets the conditions for independence set out in Chapter VI.

## Key Obligations
- Churches and religious associations may continue applying their own pre-existing comprehensive data protection rules, provided they are brought into line with the GDPR.
- Such entities must be subject to oversight by an independent supervisory authority, which may be a body specific to those churches/associations.
- The specific supervisory authority must fulfil the conditions for independence laid down in Chapter VI of the GDPR.

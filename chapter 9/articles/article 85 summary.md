# Article 85 — Processing and Freedom of Expression and Information

**Chapter:** 9 — Provisions Relating to Specific Processing Situations

## Summary
Article 85 requires Member States to reconcile the right to personal data protection with freedom of expression and information — including journalism, academic research, artistic, and literary expression. Member States must enact laws that provide for exemptions or derogations from key GDPR chapters where necessary to protect these freedoms. Each Member State must notify the Commission of the laws it adopts under this article.

## Key Obligations
- Member States must by law balance data protection rights with freedom of expression and information.
- For journalistic, academic, artistic, or literary purposes, Member States shall provide exemptions or derogations from Chapters II (principles), III (data subject rights), IV (controller/processor), V (transfers), VI (supervisory authorities), VII (cooperation and consistency), and IX (specific processing situations) where necessary.
- Each Member State must notify the Commission of laws adopted under paragraph 2, and of any subsequent amendments without delay.

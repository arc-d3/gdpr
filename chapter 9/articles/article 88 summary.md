# Article 88 — Processing in the Context of Employment

**Chapter:** 9 — Provisions Relating to Specific Processing Situations

## Summary
Article 88 authorises Member States to enact more specific rules — through law or collective agreements — for processing employees' personal data across the employment lifecycle, from recruitment through termination. Any such rules must include suitable measures to safeguard human dignity, legitimate interests, and fundamental rights of employees, with particular attention to transparency, intra-group data transfers, and workplace monitoring.

## Key Obligations
- Member States may provide more specific rules for processing employees' personal data in the employment context (recruitment, contract performance, management, equality, health and safety, property protection, termination, etc.).
- Rules must include safeguards for human dignity, legitimate interests, and fundamental rights, covering transparency, intra-group transfers, and monitoring systems.
- Member States must notify the Commission of adopted provisions by 25 May 2018, and of any subsequent amendments without delay.

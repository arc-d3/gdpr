# Article 89 — Safeguards and Derogations Relating to Processing for Archiving Purposes in the Public Interest, Scientific or Historical Research Purposes or Statistical Purposes

**Chapter:** 9 — Provisions Relating to Specific Processing Situations

## Summary
Article 89 sets out the framework for processing personal data for archiving, scientific/historical research, and statistical purposes. Such processing must apply appropriate safeguards (including data minimisation and, where possible, pseudonymisation). Member States may derogate from certain data subject rights (access, rectification, restriction, objection, and data portability) where exercising those rights would seriously impair or make impossible the achievement of these specific purposes.

## Key Obligations
- Processing for archiving, research, or statistical purposes must include appropriate safeguards including technical and organisational measures, in particular data minimisation and pseudonymisation where possible.
- Where identification of data subjects is not necessary for the purpose, processing shall be carried out in a way that no longer permits such identification.
- For scientific/historical research or statistics, Member States may derogate from Articles 15, 16, 18, and 21 where strict compliance would seriously impair the purpose.
- For public-interest archiving, Member States may additionally derogate from Articles 19, and 20 under the same conditions.
- Derogations apply only to the extent necessary for the specific purpose.

# Article 87 — Processing of the National Identification Number

**Chapter:** 9 — Provisions Relating to Specific Processing Situations

## Summary
Article 87 allows Member States to set specific conditions for processing national identification numbers or other identifiers of general application. Such identifiers may only be used under appropriate safeguards to protect the rights and freedoms of data subjects in line with the GDPR.

## Key Obligations
- Member States may define specific conditions for processing national identification numbers or equivalent general identifiers.
- Use of such identifiers must be accompanied by appropriate safeguards for data subjects' rights and freedoms.
- Processing must remain compliant with the broader requirements of the GDPR.

# Article 90 — Obligations of Secrecy

**Chapter:** 9 — Provisions Relating to Specific Processing Situations

## Summary
Article 90 allows Member States to adopt specific rules limiting supervisory authorities' investigatory and access powers (under Article 58(1)(e) and (f)) in relation to controllers or processors bound by professional secrecy or equivalent obligations. Such rules are limited to data received in the course of activities covered by the secrecy obligation and must be necessary and proportionate to reconcile data protection with the duty of secrecy.

## Key Obligations
- Member States may restrict supervisory authority powers (Article 58(1)(e) and (f)) against entities subject to professional or equivalent secrecy obligations.
- Restrictions are limited to personal data received or obtained in activities covered by the secrecy obligation.
- Any such rules must be necessary and proportionate to balance data protection with the obligation of secrecy.
- Member States must notify the Commission of adopted rules by 25 May 2018, and of any subsequent amendments without delay.

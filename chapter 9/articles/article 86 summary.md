# Article 86 — Processing and Public Access to Official Documents

**Chapter:** 9 — Provisions Relating to Specific Processing Situations

## Summary
Article 86 addresses the tension between the right of public access to official documents and the right to personal data protection. Personal data held in official documents by public authorities or bodies (or private bodies performing public-interest tasks) may be disclosed in accordance with Union or Member State law, provided such law reconciles public access with data protection requirements under the GDPR.

## Key Obligations
- Public authorities and bodies may disclose personal data from official documents where Union or Member State law so permits.
- Disclosure must reconcile the principle of public access to official documents with the right to personal data protection.
- No blanket exemption is created — national law must strike the appropriate balance on a case-by-case or category basis.

# Article 58 — Powers

**Chapter:** 6 — Independent Supervisory Authorities

## Summary
Article 58 grants every supervisory authority three categories of powers: **investigative**, **corrective**, and **authorisation/advisory**. Investigative powers include ordering information disclosure, conducting audits, accessing premises and data. Corrective powers range from warnings and reprimands to temporary or permanent bans on processing and administrative fines (Article 83). Advisory and authorisation powers allow SAs to approve codes of conduct, accredit certification bodies, adopt standard clauses, and approve binding corporate rules. All powers are subject to appropriate safeguards including judicial review. Member States may grant additional powers provided they do not impair the Chapter 7 cooperation framework.

## Key Obligations
- Exercise all three tiers of powers: investigative, corrective, and advisory/authorisation.
- Apply appropriate safeguards (effective judicial remedy, due process) to the exercise of all powers.
- Bring GDPR infringements to judicial authorities and commence legal proceedings where national law so provides.
- Additional Member State-granted powers must not impair effective operation of Chapter 7 (cooperation and consistency).

## Key Rights / Definitions
- **Administrative fines**: Monetary penalties imposed directly by the SA under Article 83 — up to €10 million or 2% of global annual turnover (lower tier), or up to €20 million or 4% of global annual turnover (upper tier), whichever is higher.
- **Ban on processing**: A corrective measure imposing a temporary or definitive prohibition on specific processing activities.
- **Accreditation**: SA authorisation of a certification body to issue GDPR certifications under Article 43.

## Applies To
Supervisory authorities, controllers, processors, certification bodies, data subjects.

# Article 55 — Competence

**Chapter:** 6 — Independent Supervisory Authorities

## Summary
Article 55 defines the general territorial competence of each supervisory authority: every SA is competent for all tasks assigned to it and all powers conferred on it within the territory of its own Member State. A specific carve-out applies to public-interest and legal-obligation processing (Article 6(1)(c) and (e)), where the SA of the relevant Member State is competent and the lead authority mechanism of Article 56 does not apply. Additionally, SAs have no competence to supervise courts when they are acting in their judicial capacity, preserving the separation of powers.

## Key Obligations
- Each SA is competent for performing all GDPR tasks and powers **within its own Member State's territory**.
- For processing by public authorities or private bodies acting under Article 6(1)(c) or (e), the competent SA is that of the Member State concerned, and Article 56 (lead authority) does not apply.
- SAs are **not competent** to supervise processing operations of courts acting in their judicial capacity.

## Key Rights / Definitions
- **Territorial competence**: The geographic scope of an SA's jurisdiction — limited to the territory of the Member State that established it.
- **Judicial capacity exception**: Courts exercising judicial functions fall outside SA oversight to protect judicial independence.

## Applies To
Supervisory authorities, controllers, processors, Member States.

# Article 53 — General Conditions for the Members of the Supervisory Authority

**Chapter:** 6 — Independent Supervisory Authorities

## Summary
Article 53 governs the appointment and dismissal of the individual members who lead each supervisory authority. Members must be appointed through a transparent process by a parliament, government, head of State, or an independent body designated under national law. They must have the qualifications, experience, and skills — especially in data protection — required to carry out their duties. A member's term ends on expiry, resignation, or compulsory retirement. Dismissal is strictly limited to cases of serious misconduct or the loss of conditions required for the role, thereby protecting SA members from politically motivated removal.

## Key Obligations
- Members must be appointed via a **transparent procedure** by the national parliament, government, head of State, or an independent body designated in national law.
- Members must possess the **qualifications, experience, and skills** — particularly in data protection — required for the role.
- A member's duties end on expiry of term, resignation, or compulsory retirement.
- Dismissal is permitted **only** for serious misconduct or if the member no longer meets the conditions required for the role.

## Key Rights / Definitions
- **Transparent appointment**: The process must be publicly accountable to prevent political capture of the supervisory authority.
- **Serious misconduct**: The sole ground (together with loss of eligibility conditions) on which a member may be dismissed before the end of their term.

## Applies To
Member States, supervisory authorities, SA members.

# Article 52 — Independence

**Chapter:** 6 — Independent Supervisory Authorities

## Summary
Article 52 establishes the principle of full independence for supervisory authorities. Each SA must act with complete independence when performing its tasks and exercising its powers, and its members must be free from all direct and indirect external influence. Members are prohibited from engaging in incompatible occupations during their term. Member States carry positive obligations to fund and resource SAs adequately — providing staff, premises, infrastructure, and a separate public budget — so that SAs can fulfil their mandate without dependency on government or industry.

## Key Obligations
- Each SA must act with **complete independence** in performing all tasks and exercising all powers under the GDPR.
- SA members must remain free from **any external influence** (direct or indirect) and must neither seek nor accept instructions from any body.
- SA members must refrain from incompatible actions and occupations for the duration of their term.
- Member States must provide each SA with the **human, technical, and financial resources**, premises, and infrastructure necessary for effective performance, including activities in the context of mutual assistance, cooperation, and Board participation.
- Each SA must be able to choose and direct its **own staff** exclusively.
- Each SA must be subject to financial control that **does not impair its independence**, with a separate public annual budget.

## Key Rights / Definitions
- **Independence**: Freedom from external instruction, influence, or direction — both formal (direct orders) and informal (indirect pressure).

## Applies To
Member States, supervisory authorities, SA members and staff.

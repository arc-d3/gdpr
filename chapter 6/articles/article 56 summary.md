# Article 56 — Competence of the Lead Supervisory Authority

**Chapter:** 6 — Independent Supervisory Authorities

## Summary
Article 56 introduces the "one-stop-shop" mechanism for cross-border processing. The supervisory authority of the Member State where a controller or processor has its main establishment (or sole establishment) acts as the lead supervisory authority for any cross-border processing that organisation carries out. This lead SA is the primary contact point for the controller or processor and coordinates with other concerned SAs under the Article 60 cooperation procedure. However, local SAs retain the right to handle complaints or infringements that relate exclusively to a local establishment or that substantially affect data subjects only in their Member State — subject to the lead SA's right to take over within three weeks.

## Key Obligations
- The SA of the controller's or processor's **main establishment** is the lead supervisory authority for cross-border processing (subject to Article 55).
- Local SAs may handle complaints or infringements relating **only to a local establishment** or **substantially affecting only local data subjects**.
- When a local SA handles such a case, it must **inform the lead SA without delay**.
- The lead SA has **three weeks** to decide whether to take over the case under Article 60; it must consider whether the controller has an establishment in the informing SA's Member State.
- If the lead SA takes the case, Article 60 applies; the informing SA may submit a draft decision.
- If the lead SA declines, Articles 61 and 62 (mutual assistance and joint operations) apply.
- The **lead SA is the sole interlocutor** for the controller or processor regarding cross-border processing.

## Key Rights / Definitions
- **Main establishment**: For controllers, the place of central administration in the EU, or the establishment where decisions on processing purposes and means are taken. For processors, the place of central administration in the EU.
- **Lead supervisory authority**: The SA competent to act as primary authority for cross-border processing under the one-stop-shop mechanism.
- **Cross-border processing**: Processing in more than one Member State, or processing that substantially affects data subjects in more than one Member State.

## Applies To
Supervisory authorities, controllers, processors.

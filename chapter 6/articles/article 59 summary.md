# Article 59 — Activity Reports

**Chapter:** 6 — Independent Supervisory Authorities

## Summary
Article 59 requires every supervisory authority to draw up an annual report on its activities. The report may include a list of types of infringement that were notified to the SA and the types of corrective measures taken under Article 58(2). This report must be transmitted to the national parliament, the government, and any other authorities designated under Member State law. It must also be made publicly available and forwarded to both the European Commission and the European Data Protection Board. This transparency obligation ensures democratic accountability and enables cross-EU oversight of supervisory activity.

## Key Obligations
- Each SA must prepare an **annual activity report**.
- The report may include: types of infringements notified and types of corrective measures taken (Article 58(2)).
- The report must be transmitted to: national parliament, government, and other designated national authorities.
- The report must be made **available to the public**.
- The report must be sent to: the **European Commission** and the **European Data Protection Board**.

## Key Rights / Definitions
- **Annual activity report**: A public accountability document summarising the supervisory authority's activities, enforcement actions, and priorities for the reporting year.

## Applies To
Supervisory authorities.

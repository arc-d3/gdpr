# Art. 46 GDPR – Transfers Subject to Appropriate Safeguards

## Summary

Article 46 provides the primary transfer toolkit for situations where **no adequacy decision exists**. Transfers are permitted if the controller or processor implements appropriate safeguards ensuring that data subjects retain enforceable rights and effective legal remedies. Safeguards are split into two tiers: those that can be used without supervisory authority authorisation and those that require it.

| Safeguard | Authorisation Required? |
|-----------|------------------------|
| Legally binding and enforceable instrument between public authorities or bodies | No |
| Binding corporate rules (Art. 47) | No (but BCRs themselves require approval) |
| Standard contractual clauses (SCCs) adopted by the Commission | No |
| Standard contractual clauses adopted by a supervisory authority and approved by the Commission | No |
| Approved code of conduct (Art. 40) + binding commitments of importer | No |
| Approved certification mechanism (Art. 42) + binding commitments of importer | No |
| Bespoke contractual clauses (controller/processor ↔ recipient) | **Yes** |
| Provisions in administrative arrangements between public authorities | **Yes** |

**Consistency mechanism**: Where supervisory authority authorisation is required, the consistency mechanism (Art. 63) must be applied.

**Transitional validity**: Authorisations granted under Directive 95/46/EC and Commission decisions on SCCs under that Directive remain valid until amended, replaced, or repealed.

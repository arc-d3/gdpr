# Art. 47 GDPR – Binding Corporate Rules

## Summary

Article 47 defines the requirements for **Binding Corporate Rules (BCRs)** — the transfer mechanism designed for multinational corporate groups and groups of enterprises engaged in a joint economic activity. BCRs allow intra-group transfers of personal data to group members in third countries without requiring SCCs for each individual transfer. The competent supervisory authority approves BCRs through the **consistency mechanism** (Art. 63).

| Requirement (Art. 47(1)) | Detail |
|--------------------------|--------|
| **Legally binding** | Apply to and are enforced by every group member, including employees. |
| **Enforceable rights** | Expressly confer enforceable rights on data subjects. |
| **Content requirements** | Must fulfil the detailed list in Art. 47(2). |

**Minimum content of BCRs (Art. 47(2)):**

| Element | Requirement |
|---------|-------------|
| Structure & contacts | Group structure and contact details for each member. |
| Data transfers | Categories of data, processing types, purposes, data subjects, destination countries. |
| Legally binding nature | Both internal and external. |
| Data protection principles | Purpose limitation, data minimisation, storage limits, data quality, data protection by design, legal basis, special categories, security, onward transfer restrictions. |
| Data subject rights | All applicable rights, including right against automated decision-making (Art. 22), right to lodge complaints (Art. 79), and right to redress/compensation. |
| Liability | EU-established controller/processor accepts liability for breaches by non-EU group members (rebuttable where the member is not responsible). |
| Transparency | How BCR information is provided to data subjects in addition to Art. 13/14 notices. |
| DPO tasks | Role of the DPO or compliance monitor; training; complaint handling. |
| Complaint procedures | Internal procedures for data subjects. |
| Compliance verification | Audits and corrective action mechanisms; results shared with the compliance monitor and board. |
| Change notification | Mechanisms for reporting and recording changes to BCRs and notifying the supervisory authority. |
| Supervisory cooperation | Mechanism for supervisory authority to verify compliance; results of audits available on request. |
| Legal requirement reporting | Mechanism for reporting legal requirements in third countries that could adversely affect BCR guarantees. |
| Training | Data protection training for staff with regular access to personal data. |

**Commission powers**: The Commission may specify formats and procedures for BCR information exchange (implementing acts, Art. 93(2)).

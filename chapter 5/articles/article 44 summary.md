# Art. 44 GDPR – General Principle for Transfers

## Summary

Article 44 is the **gateway provision** for all transfers of personal data to third countries or international organisations. It establishes the non-derogable rule that every transfer — including onward transfers from a third country to another third country — must comply with the conditions set out in Chapter 5. The article makes explicit that none of the provisions in the chapter may be applied in a way that undermines the level of protection guaranteed by the GDPR.

| Key Point | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Scope** | All transfers of personal data undergoing processing or intended for processing after transfer to a third country or international organisation. |
| **Onward transfers** | Applies equally to onward transfers from a third country or international organisation to another third country or international organisation. |
| **Non-derogable floor** | The level of GDPR protection must not be undermined — Chapter 5 tools are a floor, not a ceiling. |
| **Applicability** | Applies to both controllers and processors. |

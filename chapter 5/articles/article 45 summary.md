# Art. 45 GDPR – Transfers on the Basis of an Adequacy Decision

## Summary

Article 45 allows personal data to flow freely to a third country, territory, sector, or international organisation that the **European Commission has determined provides an adequate level of data protection**. No additional authorisation is required. The Commission must continuously monitor adequacy decisions and may repeal, amend, or suspend them if the standard is no longer met.

| Requirement | Detail |
|-------------|--------|
| **Transfer permission** | Transfers may take place without specific authorisation where an adequacy decision is in force. |
| **Adequacy criteria** | Rule of law; human rights and fundamental freedoms; relevant legislation (general and sectoral); data protection rules; independent supervisory authority with enforcement powers; international commitments. |
| **Adoption procedure** | Commission adopts adequacy decisions via implementing acts (examination procedure, Art. 93(2)). |
| **Periodic review** | At least every **four years**; considers all relevant developments in the third country or organisation. |
| **Revocation** | Commission may repeal, amend, or suspend a decision if the adequate standard is no longer ensured; repeal is without retroactive effect. |
| **Transitional validity** | Adequacy decisions under Directive 95/46/EC remain valid until amended, replaced, or repealed. |
| **Publication** | Commission publishes a list of countries/territories/sectors with adequacy in the Official Journal and on its website. |

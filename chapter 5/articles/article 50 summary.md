# Art. 50 GDPR – International Cooperation for the Protection of Personal Data

## Summary

Article 50 places obligations on the **European Commission and supervisory authorities** to actively develop international cooperation in the field of personal data protection. It is an outward-facing provision designed to strengthen global data protection standards and facilitate cross-border enforcement collaboration.

| Obligation | Detail |
|------------|--------|
| **(a) Cooperation mechanisms** | Develop international cooperation mechanisms to facilitate the effective enforcement of data protection legislation. |
| **(b) Mutual assistance** | Provide international mutual assistance in enforcement, including through notification, complaint referral, investigative assistance, and information exchange — subject to appropriate safeguards for personal data and other fundamental rights. |
| **(c) Stakeholder engagement** | Engage relevant stakeholders in discussions and activities aimed at furthering international cooperation in data protection enforcement. |
| **(d) Exchange and documentation** | Promote the exchange and documentation of personal data protection legislation and practice, including on jurisdictional conflicts with third countries. |

**Actors**: The Commission and supervisory authorities jointly bear these obligations; there are no direct obligations on controllers or processors under this article.

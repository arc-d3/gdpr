# Article 96 — Relationship with Previously Concluded Agreements

**Chapter:** 11 — Final Provisions

## Summary
Article 96 provides a continuity clause for international agreements concluded by Member States before 24 May 2016 (the date GDPR entered into force) that involve the transfer of personal data to third countries or international organisations. Such agreements remain valid as long as they complied with Union law applicable at the time of conclusion — until they are amended, replaced, or revoked.

## Key Obligations
- International data transfer agreements concluded by Member States prior to 24 May 2016 that complied with Union law as applicable prior to that date shall remain in force.
- Such agreements remain valid until they are amended, replaced, or revoked.
- Member States and organisations relying on these agreements should assess whether updates are needed to bring them into alignment with current GDPR standards.

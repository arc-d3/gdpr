# Article 97 — Commission Reports

**Chapter:** 11 — Final Provisions

## Summary
Article 97 establishes a periodic review mechanism for GDPR. The European Commission must submit a public evaluation report to the European Parliament and the Council by 25 May 2020, and every four years thereafter. The reports focus especially on international data transfers (Chapter V) and the cooperation/consistency mechanism (Chapter VII). The Commission may request information from Member States and supervisory authorities to inform these evaluations, and must submit legislative amendment proposals if needed.

## Key Obligations
- The Commission shall submit an evaluation and review report by 25 May 2020 and every four years thereafter; all reports must be made public.
- The Commission shall specifically examine the application and functioning of Chapter V (transfers to third countries) and Chapter VII (cooperation and consistency).
- The Commission may request information from Member States and supervisory authorities for evaluation purposes.
- The Commission shall take into account positions and findings of the European Parliament, the Council, and other relevant bodies.
- The Commission shall submit amendment proposals if appropriate, particularly in light of developments in information technology and the information society.

# Article 99 — Entry into Force and Application

**Chapter:** 11 — Final Provisions

## Summary
Article 99 is the final provision of GDPR. It specifies the two key dates: GDPR entered into force on the twentieth day after its publication in the Official Journal of the European Union (4 May 2016 + 20 days = 24 May 2016), and it became applicable — i.e., legally binding and enforceable — on 25 May 2018. The two-year gap between entry into force and application gave organisations and Member States time to prepare.

## Key Obligations
- GDPR entered into force on the twentieth day following publication in the Official Journal of the European Union (entered into force: 24 May 2016).
- GDPR applies from 25 May 2018 — the date from which all obligations and enforcement became fully effective.

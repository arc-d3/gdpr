# Article 98 — Review of Other Union Legal Acts on Data Protection

**Chapter:** 11 — Final Provisions

## Summary
Article 98 tasks the European Commission with reviewing other Union legal acts that deal with data protection and, where appropriate, submitting legislative proposals to amend them. The goal is to ensure uniform and consistent protection of natural persons across all Union law — with particular focus on the processing rules applicable to Union institutions, bodies, offices, and agencies, and the free movement of personal data within those frameworks.

## Key Obligations
- The Commission shall, if appropriate, submit legislative proposals to amend other Union legal acts on the protection of personal data, to ensure uniform and consistent protection.
- The review shall particularly concern rules relating to the protection of natural persons with regard to processing by Union institutions, bodies, offices, and agencies, and on the free movement of such data.

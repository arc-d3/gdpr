# Article 94 — Repeal of Directive 95/46/EC

**Chapter:** 11 — Final Provisions

## Summary
Article 94 formally repeals Directive 95/46/EC (the previous EU data protection directive) with effect from 25 May 2018 — the date GDPR became applicable. All references in existing legal acts to the repealed Directive are automatically redirected to GDPR, and references to the former Article 29 Working Party are redirected to the newly established European Data Protection Board (EDPB).

## Key Obligations
- Directive 95/46/EC is repealed with effect from 25 May 2018.
- Any reference in other Union or national legal acts to Directive 95/46/EC shall be construed as a reference to GDPR.
- References to the Working Party on the Protection of Individuals (established by Article 29 of Directive 95/46/EC) shall be construed as references to the European Data Protection Board established by GDPR.

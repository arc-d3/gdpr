# Article 95 — Relationship with Directive 2002/58/EC

**Chapter:** 11 — Final Provisions

## Summary
Article 95 prevents GDPR from creating duplicate or conflicting obligations for providers of publicly available electronic communications services (e.g., telecom operators, internet service providers). Where such providers are already subject to equivalent obligations under Directive 2002/58/EC (the ePrivacy Directive), GDPR does not layer additional obligations on top. This article ensures coherence between the two legal frameworks.

## Key Obligations
- GDPR shall not impose additional obligations on natural or legal persons in relation to processing connected with the provision of publicly available electronic communications services in public communication networks in the Union.
- This exemption applies only where those persons are already subject to specific obligations with the same objective set out in Directive 2002/58/EC.
- Providers must still comply with GDPR for any processing activities that fall outside the scope of Directive 2002/58/EC.

# Article 93 — Committee Procedure

**Chapter:** 10 — Delegated Acts and Implementing Acts

## Summary
Article 93 establishes the committee procedure through which the European Commission exercises its implementing powers under the GDPR. The Commission is assisted by a committee operating under Regulation (EU) No 182/2011 (the comitology regulation). Depending on the type of implementing act, the committee applies either the examination procedure (Article 5 of Reg. 182/2011) or the urgency procedure (Article 8 read with Article 5).

## Key Points
- **Committee assistance**: The Commission must be assisted by a dedicated committee for adopting implementing acts under the GDPR.
- **Comitology framework**: The committee operates within the meaning of Regulation (EU) No 182/2011, which governs how EU committees advise the Commission.
- **Examination procedure (paragraph 2)**: Used for standard implementing acts — the committee provides a formal opinion and the Commission may only proceed if a qualified majority supports the act.
- **Urgency/special procedure (paragraph 3)**: Article 8 of Reg. 182/2011 (together with Article 5) applies where urgency is invoked, allowing the Commission to adopt immediately applicable implementing acts in exceptional circumstances.
